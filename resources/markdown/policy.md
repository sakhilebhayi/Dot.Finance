This Privacy Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our"), the company responsible for Dot.Finance, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use Dot.Finance and the wider Dot Ecosystem it connects to. It is written to align with South Africa's **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**.

## 1. Who we are

BluePin Inc is the responsible party for the personal information Dot.Finance processes. Our Information Officer can be reached at privacy@infodot.co.za for any question, request, or concern about your personal information.

## 2. What we collect

**Account information** — your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), via your Dot Ecosystem account.

**Financial data you enter** — the accounts you add (name, type, institution, currency, balance), the transactions you record (description, amount, date, notes), the categories you create, and the budgets you set. This data is entered manually by you — Dot.Finance does not link to any bank or financial institution, and does not import or sync transactions automatically.

**Technical information** — IP address, browser and device information, and session activity, collected automatically for security and to keep you signed in.

## 3. Why we process your information

We process personal information to:

- create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you sign in;
- let you sign in once and move between connected Dot Ecosystem platforms without re-entering your credentials;
- store, display, and let you organise your accounts, transactions, categories, and budgets; and
- keep Dot.Finance secure and prevent abuse.

## 4. AI-generated insights

Dot.Finance's infrastructure is configured to use Anthropic's Claude models to generate spending insights from your transaction data in the future. **This feature is not yet active** — no transaction or financial data is currently sent to Anthropic or any other AI provider, and no insights are currently generated. If and when this feature launches, this Policy will be updated first to describe exactly what data is sent and how it's used.

## 5. Ecosystem single sign-on

When you use another Dot Ecosystem platform to sign in to Dot.Finance (or vice versa), a short-lived, single-use authentication token confirms who you are without exposing your password to the connected platform.

## 6. Who can see your financial data

Dot.Finance is a single-user platform today: your accounts, transactions, categories, and budgets are private to you and are not shared with other users, teams, or platforms. We do not sell your financial data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

## 7. How long we keep your information

We keep your account and financial data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, and any AI-insight records are permanently deleted, except where we're required by law to retain certain records for longer.

## 8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your financial data, including encrypted password storage, scoped session-based authentication, and single-use SSO tokens for ecosystem sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

- request access to the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- request deletion of your personal information;
- object to our processing of your personal information in certain circumstances; and
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@infodot.co.za.

## 10. Cookies

Dot.Finance uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you directly.

## 12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, contact us at privacy@infodot.co.za.
