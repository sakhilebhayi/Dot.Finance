# R-44 decisions (agent ranges)

The architecture decisions D-1..D-20 are in `R44_ARCHITECTURE.md` section 7. Each agent appends its own range below.

## F3 Controls: D-300..D-399

- **D-300** `Capability` has the 24 specified capabilities plus three legacy structure capabilities (`edit-structure`, `destroy-structure`, `adjust-opening-balance`) so `canEditStructure`, `canDestroyStructure` and `canAdjustOpeningBalance` keep their exact old meaning and still ask `can()`.
- **D-301** Roles are derived, not stored, in this order: the company's local owner is `owner`; otherwise InfoDot's synced team role gives the baseline (admin -> `finance_manager`, editor -> `bookkeeper`, viewer or anything unknown -> `viewer`); then a local grant (`ledger_member_roles`) may refine it. Personal ledger: the person is `owner`, no grants exist.
- **D-302** InfoDot is the ceiling. A local grant may only be one of `FinanceRole::grantable()` for the person's baseline: viewer -> {viewer, approver}; bookkeeper/accountant baseline -> {approver, viewer, bookkeeper, accountant}; finance manager baseline -> up to finance manager. Nobody is ever granted `owner` locally (also a DB CHECK). `LedgerAccess` ignores a grant above the ceiling when it reads, `LedgerRoles::reconcile` deletes it when the InfoDot sync lowers someone, and the sync never creates or raises one.
- **D-303** Only people holding `manage-members` (owner, finance manager) assign roles; never above the grantor's own rank; never change someone who outranks you; the company owner's role cannot be changed. Two finance managers are peers and may change each other's local role (the owner can always repair it).
- **D-304** Approver ranks just above viewer (a lateral role that approves but writes nothing); accountant does not hold any `approve-*` capability (independence of preparer and approver). View-audit is granted to accountants (new role; the three legacy roles are unchanged).
- **D-305** `LedgerAccess::can(Capability, ?User)` is an instance method (the single source). With a user argument it answers for that other person in the same ledger. `LedgerAccess::allows()` is the static convenience for the signed-in person. The accounting module's `canPost`, `canManageChart`, `canClosePeriod` (appended at the end of the class by F1) must delegate to `can(Capability::PostJournal | ManageChart | ClosePeriod)`; they are not defined by Controls to avoid a duplicate-method conflict.
- **D-306** Approval policy amounts: approval is needed when `amount > threshold_cents` (strict); a NULL threshold means every amount including no amount. A policy with a threshold has a currency; an amount in another currency cannot be compared and therefore needs approval (fail closed). Callers pass the ledger base-currency amount. `requiresApproval()` throws when there is no active ledger instead of answering "no".
- **D-307** A request copies the capability, minimum role, step count and self-approval flag from its policy, so editing or deleting a policy never changes a request in flight.
- **D-308** Separation of duties: nobody decides their own request, except the only member of a ledger (personal ledger, or a company whose only member is that person) when the policy sets `allow_self_approval`. It is evaluated at decision time, so the rule returns the moment a second member joins. Presets set the flag automatically in a one-member ledger so a solo owner is not deadlocked.
- **D-309** Multi-step approval (1..3) needs different people per step. A sole member's single approval completes all steps. A rejection at any step rejects the request.
- **D-310** Idempotency: the request row is locked and re-read at decision time (stale copies are never trusted); the same outcome again is a no-op that fires nothing; the opposite outcome is `already_decided`; a cancelled request is `not_pending`; a person approving a step twice is a no-op; one decision per step is a database UNIQUE; one pending request per subject is a partial UNIQUE index. Requesting again for the same amount returns the open request; for a changed amount the old pending request is cancelled and a new one made.
- **D-311** Spend limits: per person or per role, per currency, per approval / day / week (ISO) / month. A person's own rows replace their role's. Totals count the person's approved decisions on pending-or-approved requests. A person with limits but none in the request's currency cannot approve that currency (fail closed). No rows means no limit (default).
- **D-312** Modules integrate through `App\Contracts\Approvable`: `onApprovalDecided()` runs inside the decision transaction (throw to roll the decision back) and `App\Events\ApprovalDecided` is dispatched after commit; `ApprovalService::isApproved($subject)` must be checked before the guarded step and is false if the amount changed since approval.
- **D-313** Removing a member (Jetstream `TeamMemberRemoved`, or InfoDot sync detach) deletes their local role and spend limits and cancels the pending requests they asked for. What they could have approved is already out of their hands (no access, no capability).
- **D-314** Audit: policies and spend limits go through the generic `FinanceAuditObserver`; role grants, approval requests and access reviews are logged explicitly with action, actor (`FinanceAudit::record` gained an optional `actorUserId`), and a context (`member:<id>`, `approval:<kind>`). Free text (reasons, notes, policy names) is logged as "changed", never the words.
- **D-315** The app does not record sign-ins. The access review shows the latest activity of a still-stored session and the latest audit-log action by that person in this ledger, labelled as such.
- **D-316** `approval_decisions` and `access_reviews` are evidence: model guard plus a database trigger refusing UPDATE.
- **D-317** Guard tests deliberately changed: `LedgerScopeGuardTest` (model count 7 -> 13, +6 Controls ledger models; each R-44 module adds its own, the integrator sums them; DB::table allow-list gains `AccessReport` for the sessions table) and `ContextIsolationRoutesTest` (Controls routes are covered by their own cross-ledger test and skipped by the "unknown id" tripwire).

## F2 shared data layer (D-200..D-299)

- **D-200 Rounding policy is a labelled default, not a tax figure.** `R44_TAX_RESEARCH.md` contains no SARS rule for rounding invoice lines (it only notes VAT201 fields are entered in rands, rounding unconfirmed). The calculator therefore reads the rule `vat.rounding.level` (`line` or `document`), seeded for ZA as `line` with legal status `policy`, confidence `low`, and a note saying it is not a SARS rule. Its version is recorded with every answer; it does not lower the answer's confidence (that is the weakest RATE used). To change it add a new version with a later `valid_from`.
- **D-201 `policy` is a usable legal status.** Rule and rate rows carry `legal_status` in enacted, announced, policy, proposed, draft, repealed. Only enacted, announced and policy are ever resolved; proposed, draft and repealed are stored but TaxRuleBook refuses them and says why.
- **D-202 Tax code shape differs from architecture section 5.** `tax_codes.kind` is standard, zero, exempt, out_of_scope or reverse_charge (the rate class), and a separate `applies_to` (sales, purchases, both) gives the direction, because one standard-rated code serves both a sale and a purchase. Return boxes are two columns: `box_output` (where a sale with this code is reported) and `box_input` (where a purchase is). `rate_ref` names `tax_rates.rate_key`. `deductible_policy` holds the research's denied categories. No reverse-charge code is seeded: the research says imported services go to VAT201 field 12 only for non-taxable use, which depends on the vendor, so a fixed mapping would be invented.
- **D-203 Units.** Money in tax data is integer cents (`value_type=money_cents`), rates are basis points (`basis_points`, 1500 = 15 %), bands are JSON rows `{above_cents, up_to_cents, base_cents, rate_bp}`: tax is `base + rate x (income - above)` for income in `(above, up_to]`. Quantities in the calculator are strings with at most four decimals, held internally x10^4.
- **D-204 Versioned rows are append-only in the model.** TaxRule, TaxRate and TaxCode: content never changes after creation (only `valid_to`, which may only move earlier, plus review bookkeeping for rules; wording, ordering and the active flag for codes), rows are never deleted, and two versions of the same series for the same owner may not cover the same day. A ledger's own override is a row with an owner; it beats a global row for that ledger only.
- **D-205 Seed gaps are left as gaps.** Pre-2018-04-01 VAT is not seeded (the research says not to seed unverified history); the company rate ranges in the research leave 2023-03-31 uncovered and the engine refuses that date; the 2024 and 2025 individual brackets are not listed in full in the research and are not seeded; CIT 27 % is valid only to 2027-03-31 until a later version is added. Anything with no source is not stored.
- **D-206 Numbering.** Numbers are handed out at issue, per ledger and document type, inside the caller's own transaction (refused outside one). The first statement is an UPDATE that takes the row lock on both databases, then the counter is read and advanced, so a rollback gives the number back. Format `[prefix-][year-]number`. A sequence's format is frozen after its first number. A yearly sequence refuses a number for an earlier year than it has already reached.
- **D-207 Attachments.** Private disk only. Type decided from the file's content against an allow-list (PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, text, CSV, XLSX, DOCX; no HTML or SVG), 15 MB. sha256 stored; the same file in the same ledger is refused as a duplicate unless the caller allows it; re-attaching it to the same record returns the existing one. The stored path never contains the client file name; a file is never overwritten; a record is archived, never deleted. Streaming needs a temporary signed URL (10 minutes) AND the signed-in person's access to the owning record AND a ledger-scoped binding; an owning record with no policy refuses. Served as a download by default, inline only for PDFs and images, always with `nosniff` and a sandbox CSP. Extraction: status none, pending, draft, reviewed, rejected, failed; a draft is a suggestion until a person reviews it.
- **D-208 Payments.** Recorded once through `PaymentRecorder` (idempotency key unique per ledger; the same key with a different request is refused). Immutable except the links the posting modules write back (`gl_entry_id`, `transaction_id`) and the one-way move to reversed. `PaymentAllocator` holds the invariants: live allocations never exceed the payment nor the document's open balance, direction must match, same ledger, same currency (allocation across currencies is not supported in this release), same customer or supplier when both name one, a reversed payment settles nothing. The open balance is the document's total (which must already include credit notes) minus live allocations of recorded payments. Locking order is payment first, document second.
- **D-209 FX.** `rate_e8` integers; a rate is found as of a date from the entry for that day or the nearest PRIOR day, never a later one; the opposite direction is inverted; the newest day wins, a ledger's own rate beats a global one of the same day; a correction is a new row. Conversion respects each currency's minor-unit digits. Gain/loss helpers are pure functions (`FxMath::realised`, `FxMath::unrealised`).
- **D-210 ECB provider.** Verified on 2026-11-09 by fetching `https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml` (HTTP 200, 29 currencies including ZAR, one dated cube). Base EUR only, latest working day only, reference rates not tradable rates. OFF by default (`FINANCE_FX_ECB_ENABLED`), nothing schedules it, rates are parsed from text to integers.
- **D-211 Global-or-owner reference tables** use `HasOptionalLedgerContext` (global rows plus the active ledger's own, never another ledger's). `LedgerScopeGuardTest` was changed deliberately: that trait is accepted alongside `HasLedgerContext`, and the model count `7` became a floor (`>= 7`) because Wave 1 adds ledger models in parallel and the loop checks every one of them.
- **D-212 Parties.** One table for customers, suppliers and both. Archive = soft delete plus `status=archived`. Tax number and external reference are unique among a ledger's live parties (partial unique indexes), tax/registration numbers are stored without spaces. Write access uses `LedgerAccess::canRecordEntries` as a stand-in for Controls (change `PartyPolicy`, `AttachmentPolicy`, `PaymentPolicy` when capabilities arrive).
- **D-213 CHECK constraints** are written once in `App\Support\Database\TableChecks` and become a real CHECK on PostgreSQL and a pair of triggers on SQLite, like the original owner rule.
- **D-214 Wide products need bcmath.** `MoneyMath::mulDivRound` stays in plain integers while the product is safe (a product within roughly a quarter of the 64-bit range) and uses bcmath beyond that; without bcmath it throws rather than overflow.
- **D-215 Isolation test extended.** `ContextIsolationRoutesTest` now binds `party` and `attachment` and seeds one of each per ledger; every new route is walked by it.
- **D-216 Glossary.** `App\Support\Glossary`, 113 terms, South African spelling, each one sentence, with tests.
- **D-217 Team deletion.** `Team::SHARED_DATA_TABLES` makes parties, sequences, attachments, payments, allocations and a company's tax/FX overrides count as ledger data, so a company with any of them cannot be deleted. `finance:attachments:orphans` finds (and with `--delete` removes) files whose person was deleted.

## UI agent (U): D-400 to D-499

- **D-400 Concept "The Tie-Out".** An accountant verifies a figure by ticking it. Every figure can be traced to the rows behind it (the provenance mark, a real link), totals are ruled as ledgers print them (single rule subtotal, double rule total), and colour means exactly two things: ledger green = checked / ties out, pencil red = variance / overdue / over limit. Everything else is graphite on cool paper. Night is a first-class theme that follows the system until the person chooses. Recorded in `DESIGN.md`.
- **D-401 The command centre lists scheduled amounts.** The dashboard gained a "Coming up" panel (active repeating items due in the next 14 days, with their own amounts, labelled as scheduled entries) because the brief asks for money coming in and out "only when data exists". `ForecastTest::test_the_dashboard_does_not_mix_projected_figures_into_actuals` asserted the item amount was absent from the dashboard; its intent (a projected BALANCE never reaches the actual position) is kept and now asserts that the position is the actual ledger balance and that no projected balance or low point is drawn. Changed deliberately.
- **D-402 Type system.** Display: Archivo (variable, `wdth` axis, set at 112%). Text: Public Sans (variable). Figures: Chivo Mono (variable, tabular numerals, slashed zero). All three from the `@fontsource-variable` npm packages (SIL OFL 1.1), latin subset, self-hosted by Vite (about 143 KB of font files). Three families is a deliberate deviation from the design-taste-frontend skill's "two at most": the owner brief asks for a display face, a quiet text face and a tabular mono, each with one job.
- **D-403 Tailwind is kept for utilities only.** The Play CDN and preflight are gone. Tailwind 3 (the project's existing config) still compiles the utilities the stock Jetstream/Livewire views use. Colours resolve to the design tokens (RGB channel custom properties), the stock `gray`, `red`, `green`, `indigo`, `white` names are remapped to the tokens, and `dark:` variants are switched off on purpose because the tokens already flip with the theme. The look itself is plain CSS in `resources/css` (tokens, base, shell, components, pages) written as `fx-*` classes.
- **D-404 Enforced nonce CSP.** `App\Http\Middleware\SecurityHeaders`, appended in `bootstrap/app.php`: per-request nonce shared with `@vite` and the one inline theme script, `style-src 'self' 'nonce'` (no `'unsafe-inline'`), `font-src 'self'`, `img-src 'self' data: blob:`, `form-action 'self'`, `frame-ancestors 'none'`, HSTS on HTTPS only (six months, no `includeSubDomains`, no preload), nosniff, DENY, referrer and permissions policies. `'unsafe-eval'` stays in `script-src` as the one concession: Livewire 3's bundled Alpine compiles `x-data`/`x-on` expressions with `new Function()`, and the profile, team and API-token pages run on it. `SECURITY_CSP_REPORT_ONLY=true` makes the same policy report-only without a deploy; violations post to `/csp-report` (anonymous, throttled, logs only the directive and host).
- **D-405 The product map and `fx_route()`.** `App\Support\Ui\Navigation` is the single map for the sidebar, the mobile sheet and the command bar (Money, Sales, Purchases, Bank, Tax, Reports, Planning, Controls). Each destination lists route NAMES with alternatives; `fx_route()` returns the URL of the first registered one. An unregistered destination renders as a disabled item marked "Soon", never as a link. Modules add destinations with `Navigation::add()` from a provider. The canonical route names the shell is already listening for are listed in `R44_UI.md`.
- **D-406 Extension points for later agents.** `StateLine::extend(fn (LedgerContext $l) => [...])` adds an item to the state line and the "Needs you" panel; `DashboardPanels::add('view.name', order)` adds a panel to the command centre (it must draw nothing when it has no data); `<x-fx.gloss>` reads a `glossary` slot or `App\Support\Glossary` when that class exists.
- **D-407 Red is not for destructive actions.** Pencil red is reserved for a variance, an overdue item, an over-limit budget and validation errors. Delete buttons and links use ink (a heavier outline for `danger`), an icon, and always a confirmation dialog (`data-fx-confirm`), which replaces `window.confirm()`.
- **D-408 Money rendering.** `<x-fx.money>` renders one text node ("ZAR 1,234.56", so every existing text assertion still holds), brackets for negatives plus a screen-reader "negative", a transparent bracket-width on positives so decimals align, `lead`, `variance`, `ticked` and `trace` options. `<x-money>` stays as an alias; its `tone` prop is accepted and ignored (positive green / negative red is gone).
- **D-409 Jetstream clean-up.** The stock Jetstream form, modal and section components were rewritten onto the fx classes; unused stock parts were deleted (`navigation-menu`, `nav-link`, `responsive-nav-link`, `dropdown`, `dropdown-link`, `welcome`, `switchable-team`). The default avatar (a request to ui-avatars.com carrying the person's name) is replaced by a local initials avatar, and the dead profile-photo block is removed (the feature is off).
- **D-410 Two behaviours the old markup intended but never ran.** The category list now follows the chosen transaction type (the old script was bound to an id the markup shadowed with a second id, so it never ran); and `category_id=none` filters the list to entries without a category (the state line links there). Server-side validation is unchanged.
- **D-411 Public page copy.** The welcome page was rewritten: it no longer offers "Create your account" (registration is closed) and states what is not built. The old marketing roadmap block is gone.
- **D-412 Demo data is local-only.** `finance:demo-seed` builds a fictional Cape Town coffee-roastery ledger (five accounts in two currencies, about 12 months, budgets, repeating items, three uncategorised entries, one budget over its limit, one repeating item past its date) through the real models and `TransferService`. It refuses outside `local` and `testing` (production and staging), is deterministic for a given day (fixed seed, dates counted back from today), and `--fresh` clears and rebuilds the demo ledger's rows (the demo user and company are kept, because the append-only audit log points at them). The demo user's password is a published constant in the command; that is acceptable only because of the environment guard.
- **D-413 The reconciliation page is "Balance tie-out".** The route name and URL are unchanged; the title, h1 and nav label changed so it cannot be confused with the Bank module's reconciliation. Green ticks appear only there, because it is the one place a figure is actually verified a second way.
- **D-414 Mobile is one DOM.** Below 1024px the sidebar is a bottom sheet (same markup, one set of landmarks and one copy of the account controls) opened from "More" in a five-item bottom bar; at 1024px and up it is the sidebar. Tables become card lists by container query (below 760px of their own width, 480px for `fx-table--compact`), so the same table behaves the same in a half-width panel and on a phone.
- **D-415 Category default colour.** New categories default to graphite (#4b5563) instead of indigo. A user-chosen colour is only ever drawn as an SVG attribute (`<x-fx.swatch>`), never inline CSS.

Approval or submission posts at once; if approval is granted but posting fails (a closed period, no exchange rate) the document waits as `approved` with the reason in `post_error` and can be posted again or taken back to draft. The approval is never rolled back by a posting problem. | Approval is a human decision and must survive a fixable accounting problem. |
| D-602 | Input VAT is claimed only when every rule passes, in this order: tax is charged; the code is not a denied category (entertainment, motor car); the ledger is **known** to be VAT-registered (a user-provided setting, null = unknown = nothing claimed); the document shows a supplier VAT number; a valid document is held (full tax invoice, customs bill of entry, credit note; an abridged invoice only up to the full-invoice amount; no document only under the no-invoice amount); and the invoice is not outside the 5-year window. The thresholds come from the tax rule book rows. A blocked line is not an error: the whole gross goes to the expense account and the reason is kept on the line and listed ("VAT that is not being claimed"). | `R44_TAX_RESEARCH.md` 2.7 and 2.8. Claiming input VAT wrongly is the costly error, so the default is not to claim. |
| D-603 | The 5-year window is approximated (end of the invoice month plus two months plus five years) because Purchases does not know the vendor's tax period. The message says so. | The Tax module owns tax periods. |
| D-604 | A credit note linked to a bill is valued at the bill's exchange rate and takes back VAT in the same proportion the bill claimed it, line by line; it cannot be more than is still owed on the bill. An unlinked credit note must be in the ledger's own currency and can be applied to a bill later (whole credit to one bill). A credit that is applied shows as "Applied" (status `paid`); a bill fully settled by credit is `paid` too. | Keeps the payable exact without an FX difference on credits. |
| D-605 | The early-payment discount is a posted supplier credit note built from the bill's own lines (VAT inclusive), not a silent write-off. Taking it is idempotent and refused after its last day. | The VAT Act wants a credit note for a changed consideration. |
| D-606 | A payment posts Dr payable (what the paid documents were carried at) / Cr bank (what left the account) +/- realised exchange (the difference). A document's relief is its proportional share of what it is carried at (the last settlement takes the remainder, so the payable clears to the cent); the relief is stored on the allocation (`payment_allocations.base_relief_cents`, nullable = amount). A payment must be in the currency of the bills it pays (shared-layer rule D-208); a foreign payment needs a rate (typed or stored). Money left over is paid on account (a debit with the supplier) and can later be applied to a same-currency, base-currency bill without a new posting. | Exactness of the supplier ledger against the books. |
| D-607 | The cash-ledger row of a payment is created with the payment (category "Supplier payments", made on first use) and linked by `payments.transaction_id`. The cash-ledger posting leaves it alone (`SettlementTransactions`, used by `GlSync::syncTransaction`, `GlSweeper::pending`) and `TransactionPolicy` refuses to edit or delete it: reverse the payment instead. Edits to three Wave 1 files, each a few lines, are marked R-44. The Sales module needs the same rule for receipts; it works for both because it keys on `payments.transaction_id`. | Without it the money would be journalled twice, or the cash tie-out would fail. |
| D-608 | Payments above an approval threshold are not refused outright: a direct payment is turned into a one-supplier payment run and sent for approval. | D-609 | Supplier bank details are versions: encrypted at rest (`encrypted:array`), only the bank and last four digits shown, a change is a pending row until someone with `approve-payment` who is not the requester activates it (a ledger with one member may approve its own, recorded as self-approved); the first set entered by someone who may approve payments is active at once (self-approved, recorded). | D-610 | Expense claims: any member with `record-entries` (bookkeeper and above) creates and submits; `approve-expense` approves; void needs `approve-expense`. | D-611 | Receipt capture: the inbox is the set of unattached attachments.
